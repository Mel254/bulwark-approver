# Bulwark partner approver

This page is for the person who is someone's accountability partner in Bulwark. Open it on your own phone or computer:
create your key once, send the public key to your friend, and approve (or not) the requests they send you.

It runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere: no server, no analytics, no storage outside your browser.
The private key is created by your browser and can never be exported or read back, even by the page. The source is the
single `index.html` file here.
